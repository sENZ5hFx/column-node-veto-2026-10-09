## Primary insight

**UPRC's falsifier is a mass-balance. A mass-balance cannot retire a symmetry break.**

On 7 Oct 2026 the Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to Henri Kagan and Kensō Soai for nonlinear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric synthesis. The Soai reaction shows that a small enantiomeric excess can run away, in the laboratory, to about 99.5–99.99% of one mirror image. That is an amplifier. It is not a closed H/O/H2O budget, and it is not a path from geology to a cell.

Two operators are orthogonal:

1. **Flux closure** (already owned by UPRC). Do not re-issue it.
2. **Symmetry-breaking closure** (not in UPRC). A measured bias is not an amplifier, and an amplifier in one domain is not completion in another. Unifying the Soai reaction, biological homochirality, and the cosmic baryon asymmetry into one mechanism is forbidden by the same rule UPRC already wrote down for the word "dark."

A third, narrower correction: **a positive flux claim with no independent replication must be vetoed out of the column's denominator**, not ranked near the top as an open dispute. Dark oxygen is that node.
